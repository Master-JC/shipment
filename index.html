A CARRIER AND A BROKER

Freight moved across the country. No guessing who’s behind the wheel.

Route Logistics combines motor carrier and freight brokerage capabilities under one roof. When a shipment fits our equipment and lanes, we haul it. When another qualified carrier is the better fit, we arrange it with a vetted partner. Either way, you know the role before dispatch.

When we haul it

Our own equipment

Direct carrier service, a clear point of contact, and accountability from pickup through delivery.

When we place it

Vetted carrier network

Carrier identity, authority, and insurance details are checked and disclosed before the truck arrives.
